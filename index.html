STUDIO 9 · FARADAY & LENZ

Straight-wire current inducing loop current

Studio 9 Problem 3: a long straight wire carries I(t) = Qt beside a circular loop. The loop's nearest edge is b from the wire.

For d = b + R, |ε| = μ₀Q[d − √(d² − R²)] and Iind = |ε|/Rloop. For the worksheet's current direction, Lenz's law gives clockwise induced current.
